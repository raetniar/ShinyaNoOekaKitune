1. Key New Features & Updates in Ver0.4
------------------------------------------------------------
■ Full Localization Support
- A single file now supports Japanese, English, and Chinese. The tool automatically detects your browser's language and switches all text, including the UI, tooltips, and alerts.

■ Automatic Date Replacement ({date})
- If you include "{date}" in your title, it will be automatically replaced with today's date when pushed to Twitch.
- You can choose the date format (e.g., MM/DD, YYYY/MM/DD) from the Settings menu.
- A dedicated "● {date}" button in the header allows you to easily copy the replacement string.

■ Custom Tooltips
- All buttons now display descriptive tooltips on hover.
- Functionalities in the Command tab (e.g., /shoutout, /clear) are now explained at a glance.

■ UI Design & Stability Improvements
- Added a real-time clock, custom-designed dialogs (alerts), and refined color schemes for better visibility and ease of use within OBS.

2. Safe Installation Method (To prevent OBS-related errors)
------------------------------------------------------------
If you perform the initial setup directly within the OBS dock, the Twitch login screen may not open properly.
Please follow the steps below for installation:

① Save the downloaded "Title_manager_localize_Ver0.4.html" file to a permanent location on your PC.

② Double-click the file to open it in a standard browser such as Google Chrome.

③ Click the "⚙ (Settings)" button in the top-right corner to configure initial settings (Broadcaster ID, Client ID, Token, etc.).
   *Refer to the "❔ (Guide)" button within the tool for instructions.

④ After configuration, copy the data from the "Backup" tab and temporarily save it in a text editor like Notepad.

⑤ Open the OBS menu [Docks] > [Custom Browser Docks].
   Paste the path from your browser's address bar in step ② (e.g., file:///C:/Users/...) into the URL field and click Apply.

⑥ Once the tool appears in OBS, open the "Backup" tab, paste the data from step ④, and restore your settings.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Security and Backups (Important)
------------------------------------------------------------
■ Security
- Never share your "Client ID" or "Token" with others.
- Be careful not to let the "Settings" screen appear on your stream.

■ Data Storage
- Data is saved in your browser's cache (Local Storage). To prevent data loss, periodically copy your data from the "Backup" tab and save it to a text file.
